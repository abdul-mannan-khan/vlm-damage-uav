Step 10 pre-registration: trust and belief evaluation (written 12 Nov 2026, before computing any of the numbers below)

Source of the protocol: the TrustTAI checklist (sister project, sent 12 Nov) and the user's rule of 30 Oct
(calibration, coverage, risk-coverage, failure rates with Clopper-Pearson, seed variance, belief statement).

Data (fixed, already on disk, no retraining, no new inference):
  results/vlm_damage/tune/eval_sft_s{0..3}_{val,test}.jsonl  (damage question; 5 classes; test n = 450)
  results/vlm_damage/jetson/20261110_173516/{val,test,city}.jsonl  (int8 seed 0 on the Jetson)
Calibration pipeline exactly as in the paper (scripts/vlm_damage_posthoc.py, V3): per-seed vector scaling fitted on
validation half A, ensemble temperature on half A, conformal threshold on half B (alpha = 0.1), test used once.

Belief statement to be written in the paper: the belief p(c|x) is the softmax of the model's five answer-letter
logits after vector scaling; the onboard belief is fixed during a mission (no online update); the four-seed ensemble
average separates total uncertainty H(p_bar) into an aleatoric part E_s[H(p_s)] and an epistemic part (mutual
information MI = H(p_bar) - E_s[H(p_s)]). A single seed has no epistemic estimate.

Metrics (all on the 450 test images):
  M1 Brier score and its Murphy decomposition (reliability, resolution, uncertainty); NLL; ECE (15 equal-width bins,
     as in the paper) and equal-mass ECE; per seed and ensemble.
  M2 Risk-coverage curve (confidence = max calibrated probability), AURC, selective accuracy at 90 % coverage;
     single seeds and ensemble; ensemble also ranked by MI and by total entropy.
  M3 Error detection AUROC of: max probability, total entropy, aleatoric entropy, MI (ensemble).
  M4 Conformal sets, marginal and class-conditional (Mondrian): coverage, 95 % Clopper-Pearson lower bound and mean
     set size per class.
  M5 False alarm rate: images whose true class is intact or minor (or no building) but whose top label is major or
     destroyed; ensemble and the int8 Jetson seed; Clopper-Pearson 95 % upper bound.
  M6 Shift check: desktop-calibrated seed-0 sets applied to the int8 Jetson logits (coverage before re-calibration)
     and after re-calibration on Jetson validation half B.
  M7 Paired bootstrap (10,000 resamples over images) for ensemble minus mean single seed: AURC, Brier, macro-F1.

Pass bars (reported whether they pass or fail):
  H10a ensemble AURC lower than the mean single-seed AURC, paired bootstrap 95 % CI excludes 0.
  H10b MI detects errors better than chance: AUROC 95 % bootstrap CI lower bound > 0.5.
  H10c Mondrian sets: every class coverage >= 0.85 (n per class 68-129, so a CP lower bound >= 0.9 is not expected).
  H10d ensemble false alarm upper bound (CP 95 %) <= 0.05.
Not done in this step (needs a GPU, will be pre-registered separately): a general-ability probe before and after
fine-tuning (forgetting check), and an onboard four-seed ensemble on the Jetson.
Amendment (12 Nov, made AFTER the first run, because its M3 showed aleatoric > total, which is impossible; a bug, not a choice): the decomposition uses the plain seed average p_bar = mean_s p_s (no ensemble temperature), so that H(p_bar) = E_s H(p_s) + MI holds exactly; the first run mixed the two and gave aleatoric > total.
