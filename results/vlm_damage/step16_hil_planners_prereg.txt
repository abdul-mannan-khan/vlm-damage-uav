Step 16 pre-registration: the three planners on flight hardware with the VLM in the loop (written 13 Nov 2026 before
any step-16 mission; only 30 s dry runs on fake backends were run to check the code, seed 21000, not analysed:
scratchpad dry16/).

Why: the paper's own hardware-in-the-loop evidence for the planners it compares. Step 13 flew only RSG (2 UAVs). This
step flies RSG, RSG-BF and RAG on the same set-up, with fresh seeds, from this repository's code (sim/airsim_vlm/hil_vlm).
User decision 13 Nov 2026: "Go for new run for this paper".

Set-up (as step 13 unless stated): UAV 0 flown by PX4 on the Pixhawk 6C in HITL (props off, USB power), AirSim
CityEnviron on the laptop (physics, sensors, downward camera); the Jetson Orin Nano runs the network model, all UAVs'
decisions (1 s rounds) and the int8 VLM (one seed, Jetson re-calibration int8_v2_calibration.npz) on UAV 0's frame every
5 s. Changes from step 13: n = 3 UAVs (UAVs 1-2 kinematic), and VLM arm "prior" only (the closed loop of sec:hilvlm:
each frame's conformal-set midpoint weight scales the planner's prior user value within 25 m of the seen buildings,
shared with all planners, --vlm-share all).
Planners: RSG (maqac_seq, relayed list, chain rule), RSG-BF (maqac_seq_bf: RSG + best-first order, sec:bf), RAG.
Seeds 21000-21004 (not used in any earlier step), all three planners per seed, 15 missions of 300 s, complete seeds
first, one batch (no reboot with the vehicle in the air), plus a 60 s shakedown (seed 19999, not analysed).
Rig protocol: robotq booking, ~/.pixhawk.lock and Jetson ~/vlm_research/.x500.lock, the user's same-day
"props off, USB power" in chat, full same-day parameter backup, restored and read back to 0 differences,
GF 100/60/3 and SYS_HITL 0 at the end, hand-over checklist.
Command: SEEDS="21000 21001 21002 21003 21004" VLM_PLANNER=prior METHODS="maqac_seq maqac_seq_bf rag" N_UAV=3
         OUTDIR=results/hil_step16 bash hil_campaign_vlm.sh

Pass bars (fixed now; all reported, failures included):
  Q1 no failsafe, mode drop, disarm in the air or abort in any mission (k/n with Clopper-Pearson 95 % upper bound)
  Q2 0 decision-round overruns (round not finished inside its 1 s), every planner
  Q3 in-loop VLM compute p90 <= 5 s; >= 95 % of frame requests answered; 0 worker errors; belief age at use <= 10 s
  Q4 fleet decision time p95 <= 100 ms per round with the VLM running, for each planner
  Q5 system memory in use <= 7.3 GB at every round; no OOM kill
  Q6 per mission, J within +-5 % of its replay twin (same VLM weights at the same rounds, no HIL), each planner
  Q7 UAV 0 horizontal tracking error mean <= 5 m in every mission
Reported without a test (5 seeds are too few): decision time per planner (median, p95, max), messages per round, and
the paired J differences RSG-BF - RSG and RSG - RAG (mean, range, sign count). Expectation stated now: with 3 UAVs the
planners differ by well under 1 % (step 12 found the best-first gain only at 8-16 UAVs on 200 m links).
What the paper may claim: the three planners run in real time on flight hardware with the VLM in the loop, and the
hardware loop reproduces the replay twin within the stated bar. Not claimed: any ranking of planners from these
missions, any benefit of damage-aware weighting (intact city), any building-clearance figure.
